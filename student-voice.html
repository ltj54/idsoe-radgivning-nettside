THE STUDENT’S VOICE

Student participation and reflection on learning

A recurring point is that children themselves have important knowledge about how school life feels. This applies to learning, relationships and the sense of belonging.

Reflection on learning can provide a more nuanced basis for adapting teaching: when does collaboration work well? When is working alone useful? What helps the student move forward?

Questions that can start a conversation

  • What would you like to learn more about?
  • When do you really need to think hard to make progress?
  • Who do you enjoy exploring and discussing things with?
  • When do you feel your ideas are listened to?
  • What could make your school day more meaningful?

These suggestions support dialogue. They are not quotations or an assessment tool.
