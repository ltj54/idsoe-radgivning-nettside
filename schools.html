FOR SCHOOLS

Courses and guidance on bullying in schools

We offer courses, workshops and guidance for schools seeking to prevent, detect and address bullying in a safe, systematic and research-informed way. The service gives staff a shared language, greater capacity to act and practical tools for creating an inclusive and safe school environment.
