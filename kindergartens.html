FOR KINDERGARTENS

Courses and guidance on bullying behaviour in kindergarten

We offer courses, workshops and guidance for kindergartens seeking to prevent, identify and address bullying behaviour in a safe and research-informed way. The service gives staff greater understanding, a shared language and practical tools for strengthening inclusion, social competence and a safe psychosocial environment. Courses can be tailored to the whole staff group, individual departments, management or parent groups.
