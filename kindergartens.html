FOR KINDERGARTENS

Courses and guidance for kindergartens

We offer courses and guidance that help kindergarten staff understand children with high learning potential and how their abilities may be expressed through play, learning and everyday situations. You will gain practical perspectives and tools to recognise, understand and respond to these children’s needs in an inclusive way. The aim is to create a kindergarten environment where children with high learning potential receive appropriate challenges, recognition and room to develop.
