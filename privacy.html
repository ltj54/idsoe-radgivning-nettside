PRIVACY

Privacy information

This draft site does not collect information through a contact form, booking, payment or analytics.

The contact dialog

The contact dialog prepares an email locally and opens your own email application. The website does not store its contents. The dialog will be activated only after the new Gmail address for Ella has been confirmed.

Do not send information about children

Do not send sensitive or confidential information about children through ordinary email or other open contact channels.

Digital booking and report handling

The website currently has no digital booking, payment or facility for sending or receiving developmental interviews, profiles or reports. Before such functions can open, their purpose, consent, data-controller responsibility, suppliers, access, secure storage, retention and deletion must be clarified.

A separate solution for consultation and reports

Information about an identifiable child must not be collected through an ordinary website form. Guidance, developmental interviews and reports require an appropriate separate solution. An educational description of strengths and needs must not be presented as a diagnosis.

Your rights

A final contact point and complete privacy information will be published before the website receives personal data. See the Norwegian Data Protection Authority’s guidance on the right to information.

Last updated: 29 September 2026. This is a working draft, not legal advice.
