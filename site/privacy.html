PRIVACY

Privacy information

The contact form will only be activated once the business has established routines for handling and deleting enquiries.

What information may be collected?

The contact form may ask for your name, email address, subject and message. Do not send sensitive or confidential information through the form.

How is the information used?

The information is intended to read and respond to enquiries about courses, seminars or advice. The legal basis, retention period and deletion routines must be clarified with Ella.

Formspree

The form is planned to use Formspree. Before activation, the business must check Formspree’s terms, processor arrangements, storage location and deletion procedures.

Contact and rights

The business’s public email address has not been agreed in this draft. Once approved, it should be included here. You may have rights to information, access, rectification and erasure under data protection rules. See the Norwegian Data Protection Authority’s information about the right to information.
