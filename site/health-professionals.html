For doctors and psychologists

Learning potential, school experiences and a holistic assessment of functioning.

Knowledge about learning potential can help place a child’s strengths, difficulties and everyday life in context. The educational description must be kept separate from assessment of symptoms and diagnoses.

Take both strengths and difficulties seriously

Strong abilities do not rule out co-occurring difficulties. Abilities can also hide difficulties. Assessment includes developmental history, health, strengths, interests and functioning across settings. See the Directorate of Health’s guideline (in Norwegian).

Boredom, restlessness or low participation do not on their own indicate a diagnosis. Examine the factors that may explain the difficulties. Information about the learning environment is part of the context, not a substitute for clinical assessment.

High learning potential and ADHD can coexist. Better concentration during an interesting task does not by itself establish the cause of difficulties. The Norwegian Directorate of Health describes the need to assess functioning across settings, alternative explanations and co-occurring difficulties (in Norwegian).

Understand variation across situations

The child may function differently at home, at school and in activities. Interpret observations in relation to the situation and day-to-day variation. High ability can coexist with neurodevelopmental disorders. Read about assessment components (in Norwegian).

As part of collaborative reflection, professionals can explore what characterises situations in which the child participates well and what is different when they struggle. The child’s experience of tasks, relationships and belonging provides important information about school life.

Connect understanding with the child’s everyday life

A useful topic for collaboration between the family, school and relevant professionals is how support can address both learning needs and other needs. Who follows up on what, how is the child heard and how are experiences reviewed? These questions can guide collaboration without assuming that all difficulties have the same cause.

This section provides professional background for doctors and psychologists. It does not describe an assessment or treatment service offered by Idsøe Rådgivning.
