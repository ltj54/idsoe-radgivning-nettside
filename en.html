SHARING PROFESSIONAL KNOWLEDGE

Courses and seminars with Ella Idsøe

On high learning potential, differentiated teaching and inclusion.

Ella delivers courses and seminars for parents, teachers and other professionals who want to learn more about children and students with high learning potential.

The subject, audience and arrangements for a particular session are discussed with Ella.

An enquiry can describe who the session is for, which questions you would like to explore and your preferred timing.

Enquire about a course or seminar

Subject areas

  • High learning potential and differing learning needs
  • Differentiation and adapted teaching
  • Motivation and academic progression
  • Inclusion, belonging and student participation

The content of each course or seminar is agreed with Ella.

Who can the courses be adapted for?

Content and format can be adapted for school leaders, primary and lower-secondary teachers, kindergarten staff, educational-psychological services (PPT) and other professional communities. The audience, questions and practical framework are agreed with Ella before delivery.
