Our goal

We work to ensure that children and young people are seen, understood and met in a way that respects the whole person – their abilities, needs and life situation.

A particular focus of our work is children and young people with high learning potential. High learning potential is not only a strength; it can also be complex. Many children and young people with high cognitive ability feel misunderstood, excluded or inadequate. Some struggle with low self-esteem, a lack of mastery or difficulties in relationships, even though they have strong potential for learning. High learning potential does not necessarily mean consistently strong performance or that a child is able to use their resources well.

Through advice, guidance, courses and webinars, we aim to strengthen the adults around the child. Our services build on research-based knowledge and more than 30 years of practical experience. We support parents, kindergarten and school staff, and other professionals who work with children and young people in everyday life.

Our goal is for children and young people – whether they struggle to be understood, experience exclusion or face challenging relationships – to receive the right support at the right time. This can help them experience safety, belonging, mastery and positive opportunities for development.
